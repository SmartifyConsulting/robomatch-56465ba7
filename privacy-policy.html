Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 June 2026

SyncMyDay ("we", "us", or "our") operates www.syncmyday.co.za (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the rights you have over it.

Google API Services User Data Policy: SyncMyDay's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you sign in with Google or connect an email/calendar account, we collect:

  • Your name and profile picture (from Google or Microsoft)
  • Your email address(es)
  • OAuth tokens that allow us to act on your behalf

1.2 Email Data (Gmail)

To provide the unified mailbox feature, we access your Gmail account using the https://mail.google.com/ scope. This allows us to:

  • Read incoming and existing messages so they appear in your SyncMyDay inbox
  • Send emails on your behalf when you compose or reply from within SyncMyDay
  • Manage message labels, read/unread status, and trash so your actions in SyncMyDay are reflected in your Gmail account

We do not sell, share, or use your email content for advertising or any purpose other than displaying and managing your mail within SyncMyDay.

1.3 Calendar Data (Google Calendar)

To provide the unified calendar feature, we access your Google Calendar using the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar scope. This allows us to:

  • Read your existing events and calendars
  • Create, update, and delete events when you make changes in SyncMyDay
  • Send and manage calendar invitations on your behalf

Calendar data is used exclusively to populate and synchronise your SyncMyDay calendar view and is never used for advertising or sold to third parties.

1.4 Microsoft / Outlook Data

If you connect a Microsoft account, we collect the same categories of email and calendar data as described above for Gmail/Google Calendar, governed by the same use restrictions.

1.5 Usage Data

We automatically collect log data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and timestamps to maintain service reliability and improve performance. This data does not include the content of your emails or calendar events.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the SyncMyDay Service
  • To display your emails and calendar events in the unified interface
  • To send emails and calendar invitations on your behalf when you explicitly request it
  • To synchronise changes you make in SyncMyDay back to the originating Gmail or Outlook account
  • To authenticate you and maintain your session securely
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not use your Google or Microsoft user data to serve advertising, train AI or machine-learning models, or share it with any third party except as described in Section 4 (Service Providers) or as required by law.

3. Data Storage and Security

OAuth tokens are stored encrypted at rest. We transmit all data over TLS (HTTPS). Cached email and calendar data required for Service functionality is stored on secured servers and is accessible only to you and the authorised personnel needed to maintain the Service.

We retain cached email and calendar data only as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. When you disconnect an account or delete your SyncMyDay account, we delete the associated data within 30 days.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We may share data with trusted third-party vendors who assist us in operating the Service (e.g., cloud infrastructure, analytics). These providers are contractually obligated to use your data only for the purpose of providing services to us and are prohibited from disclosing it.

We do not sell your personal information to any third party.

5. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

SyncMyDay accesses Google user data only in the ways described in this Privacy Policy. Specifically:

  • Data obtained via Google APIs is used only to provide or improve user-facing features within SyncMyDay.
  • We do not allow humans to read your Google data unless you explicitly ask for help, it is necessary for security purposes, or it is required by law.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising or to train AI/ML models.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as necessary to provide the Service, with your explicit consent, or as required by law.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Revoke access to your Google or Microsoft account at any time via your Google Account settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or Microsoft account settings
  • Port your data in a machine-readable format on request
  • Object to or restrict certain types of processing

South African users also have rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@syncmyday.co.za.

7. Children's Privacy

SyncMyDay is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
